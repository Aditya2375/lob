Under the hood

  1. 01

    The book

    Price-time priority. Orders rest in FIFO queues per level; a crossing order walks the opposite side, best price first. Cancels are O(1) through an id map. Self-matches are cancelled back, never traded.

  2. 02

    The market

    A fair-value random walk. Noise takers hit or lift at random; informed takers trade toward fair value when the mid drifts. Passive background orders keep depth behind the quotes.

  3. 03

    The bot

    Quotes both sides of the mid. The spread widens with recent volatility; both quotes shift against inventory, and a hard cap stops it adding on that side. It reprices every two ticks.

  4. 04

    The accounting

    Cash plus inventory marked to the mid, every tick. Fills are attributed by counterparty and marked out against fair value 50 ticks on - that is where adverse selection shows.
